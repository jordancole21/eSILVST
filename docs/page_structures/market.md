# Market

- **Header Block**: A fixed header with the eSILVST Wallet logo on the left and a back arrow icon labeled "Back to Dashboard" on the right.
- **Price Overview Block**: A top card displaying current prices with large text "Silver Price: $X.XX/oz" and "ETH Price: $X,XXX.XX" side by side, with a small "Refresh" icon button to update values.
- **Price Trend Block**: Below the overview, a tabbed chart section with options for "24H", "7D", and "30D" views, defaulting to "24H". The chart displays silver price trends over the selected timeframe, with a toggle to overlay ETH price trends for comparison.
- **Quick Action Block**: A centered "Trade Now" button in a contrasting color below the chart, encouraging immediate action based on market data.
- **Navigation Bar Block**: A fixed bottom navigation bar with icons and labels for "Dashboard", "Trade", "Market" (highlighted), "History", and "Settings".
