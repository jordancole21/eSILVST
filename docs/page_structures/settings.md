# Settings

- **Header Block**: A fixed header with the eSILVST Wallet logo on the left and a back arrow icon labeled "Back to Dashboard" on the right.
- **Account Info Block**: A top card displaying "Connected Wallet: 0x...1234" with a small "Disconnect" button, and below it, an option to "Copy Address" for easy sharing.
- **Network Settings Block**: A section titled "Network" with a dropdown selector for "Ethereum Mainnet" or "Sepolia Testnet", showing the current selection, and a small info icon explaining network differences.
- **Security Settings Block**: A section titled "Security" with a toggle for "Session Timeout" (e.g., "After 10 minutes of inactivity") and a button for "Manage Notifications" to configure alerts.
- **Preferences Block**: A section titled "Preferences" with options like "Theme" (Light/Dark toggle) and "Currency Display" (USD/ETH selector for price views).
- **Support Links Block**: A section titled "Support" with tappable links to "About eSILVST" and "Help & Support" for additional resources.
- **Navigation Bar Block**: A fixed bottom navigation bar with icons and labels for "Dashboard", "Trade", "Market", "History", and "Settings" (highlighted).
