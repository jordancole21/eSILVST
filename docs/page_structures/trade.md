# Trade

- **Header Block**: A fixed header with the eSILVST Wallet logo on the left and a back arrow icon labeled "Back to Dashboard" on the right.
- **Tab Navigation Block**: A tabbed interface at the top with two options, "Buy eSILVST" (default selected) and "Sell eSILVST", displayed as large, tappable segments for easy switching on mobile.
- **Input Form Block (Buy Tab)**: Under the "Buy" tab, a form with a field labeled "ETH Amount" for input, showing current balance (e.g., "Available: 0.045 ETH"), and below it, a read-only field "Estimated eSILVST" (e.g., "1.12 tokens") updated dynamically based on input and current prices.
- **Input Form Block (Sell Tab)**: Under the "Sell" tab, a form with a field labeled "eSILVST Amount" for input, showing current balance (e.g., "Available: 5.23 tokens"), and below it, a read-only field "Estimated ETH" (e.g., "0.01 ETH") updated dynamically.
- **Gas Fee Estimate Block**: Below the form, a small text area showing "Estimated Gas Fee: 0.0005 ETH" with a refresh icon to update based on network conditions.
- **Confirm Action Block**: A large "Confirm Trade" button in a contrasting color below the form, centered for easy access, with a small disclaimer text underneath stating "Ensure you review amounts and fees before confirming."
- **Navigation Bar Block**: A fixed bottom navigation bar with icons and labels for "Dashboard", "Trade" (highlighted), "Market", "History", and "Settings".
